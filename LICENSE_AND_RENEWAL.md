# License And Renewal

## Available License Plans

- `7-Day Trial`: works for 7 days, usually used for demos or evaluation
- `30-Day Subscription`: works for 30 days on one machine, then needs a renewal key
- `1-Year Subscription`: works for 1 year on one machine, then needs a renewal key
- `Perpetual Lifetime`: works on one machine with no expiry

## Machine-Bound Licenses

The monthly, yearly, and perpetual licenses are tied to the `Machine ID` shown inside the app.

That means:

- the key works only on the machine it was issued for
- if the customer changes computers, they need a new key
- if the customer renews monthly or yearly, the seller issues a new key for the same `Machine ID`

## How Renewal Works

For a `30-Day Subscription` or `1-Year Subscription`:

1. The customer opens the app.
2. The customer copies the same `Machine ID` from the license screen.
3. The seller generates a fresh renewal key for that same machine.
4. The customer pastes the new key into the license screen.
5. The app replaces the old license with the new one.

The customer does not need to reinstall the app or create a new account to renew.

## If A License Expires

If a trial, monthly, or yearly key expires:

- the customer opens the app
- the app will show that the license is expired or inactive
- the customer requests a fresh key from the seller
- the seller issues a new key
- the customer pastes the new key into the activation screen

## If The Customer Changes Machine

If the customer moves to another Windows PC or Mac:

1. Open the app on the new machine.
2. Copy the new `Machine ID`.
3. Send that new `Machine ID` to the seller.
4. The seller issues a replacement key for the new machine.

## Important

- Trial keys are best for demos and evaluation
- Monthly and yearly keys are best when you want renewable subscriptions
- Perpetual keys are best for one-time lifetime sales
- Your AI, SMTP, and mail settings are separate from the desktop software license
